Questions

The ones people actually ask

Still stuck? The enquiry form is also a question box — ask in the name field and we'll reply there.

Ask a question
Do I need to know how to code?

No. Weeks 1–2 and 4 use no-code tools. Week 3 has a guided notebook where you edit six lines, not write them. If you can follow a spreadsheet formula, you can finish this.

How much time does it really take?

Four hours live plus roughly three hours of project work per week. The three-hour estimate is honest — people who try to do it in one hour produce projects that don't work.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted within four hours. But the review sessions are where most of the learning happens, so we cap absences at two.

Is the certificate worth anything?

Frankly, the certificate is the least valuable part. What gets looked at is the portfolio page with three working projects and the demo video. We say that openly because it changes how you spend your time here.

Why “pay after completion”?

Because a course that charges upfront is incentivised to enrol you, not to teach you. We'd rather lose the fee on someone who drops out than pretend everyone finishes.

Can my company pay?

Yes — pick “I'm here for my team” and we'll send a GST invoice and a one-page justification document your manager can forward.
